✦ FEATURES

The real rules,
not a toy version

A pure-Java rules engine with 362 tests. If the mod ever scores an envido wrong, you'd better start drafting an apology.

Complete rules

Envido, Real Envido, Falta Envido, Flor, Contraflor y al resto, Truco / Retruco / Vale Cuatro, ties, "envido comes first" and folding to the deck. To 15 or 30 points, with or without flor.

1v1 and team 2v2

Sit down alone, or build the Big Table (2×2): the side you sit on picks your team. During the envido round only the losing team gets to speak, just like at a real table.

Bluff your points

When the envido is accepted you declare your points out loud. Only at the end of the hand does your rival decide: "I believe you" or "I don't believe you!" (showdown) — and a caught liar pays the penalty. Works in 1v1 and 2v2.

Hand signs (and getting caught)

In 2v2 you signal your cards and points to your partner over a private channel. But watch out: alert rivals can catch your sign — a split-second flash — and you can catch theirs.

A physical table

A table block with green felt: right-click and you take a seat. Played cards show up on the table, in the world. Crafted from green wool and planks.

Anti-cheat

The server is the single authority: your rival's cards never travel to your client until they're played. Not with hacks, not by sniffing packets.

The Capybara, 4 levels

Tranqui, Canchero, Picante and the ultra-rare Messi (~3% of the time, the toughest of them all). Each with its own look, its own lines — and with the bluffing variant on, its own lies.

Pets

Beat a wild Capybara and brew it a mate until it's tamed: it follows you around, plays as your 2v2 partner via the "Set up the table" UI, and the ones left out cheer from the sidelines.

Truco out in the world

Shacks with a table and wild Capybaras, wandering Capybaras that adopt new tables, and the ultra-rare Pulpería: the countryside saloon with two small tables, a Big Table and 5 Capybaras of varying difficulty.

Kettle and energizing mate

Craft the kettle from iron, drop it on a campfire like food, and brew the mate: two sips of Regeneration and Speed. A brewed mate tames for free, and offering one to a wild Capybara lowers its distrust.

Achievements and rematch

Its own advancement tab (perfect game, showing 33, beating the Picante, taming your first pet…), a configurable Rematch at the end, and a flashy game-over screen with the final hands and the summary.

Stats and /truco last-match

Your record against the Capybara per level, bluffs slipped/caught/shown with /truco stats, and the full play-by-play of your last game, paginated, with /truco last-match.

Configurable GUI

A real 40-card Spanish deck, buttons showing only the legal calls, a matchstick scorepad and hand-by-hand narration — framed with the mod's own wooden panels. Table/card scale and camera, all adjustable.

es_AR / es_ES / en_US

UI and narration translated into all 3 languages. Reactive one-liners, grunts with meaning ("envido", "truco", "flor") and the mate round passing from hand to hand around the table.

✦ THE HOUSE RIVAL

The Capybara — 4 levels

Nobody online? Sneak and click your empty table: a mate-brewing Capybara sits down across from you, at Canchero level. But if you go roam the world you'll run into all four, each with its own look and its own way of playing — no labels, you can tell by the outfit.

Tranqui

Chews a blade of grass and wants no trouble: honest and shy, it never inflates its points.

1 MATETAME 1/2

Canchero

Gaucho scarf around its neck and a balanced game. The default rival at your own table.

2 MATESTAME 1/3

Picante

Poncho on and hot-blooded: aggressive, throws in fake flores and trusts no one. Catch it lying? It'll hold a grudge — and if it catches you, it targets you for the rest of the game.

3 MATESTAME 1/4

Messi

Argentina jersey and nobody's fool: the superstar. Extremely rare (~3%) and the hardest of the four, a step above the Picante in everything.

4 MATESTAME 1/5

Each Capybara has its own one-liners and grunts with meaning — one sounds like "envido", another like "truco", another like "flor". It brews its own mate in rounds of sips like any human player at the table.

And it remembers: catch it lying in a showdown and its distrust goes up for the rest of the game. Offer it a brewed mate, and it goes down.

✦ YOUR CREW

Pets

Beat a wild Capybara and brew it a mate: if it works out (the odds depend on its level, same as taming), it's yours. From then on it's part of your team.

Tame it with mate

Win a game against it and brew a mate to attempt the taming: 1/2 for Tranqui, 1/3 for Canchero, 1/4 for Picante and 1/5 for Messi. You get a window to claim it before it slips away.

It follows you, or stays put

By default it follows you everywhere. Tell it to "stay" and it sits down in the world to rest — it doesn't play, doesn't wander off and doesn't get lost.

Your 2v2 partner

At the Big Table, the "Set up the table" UI lets you pick which pet you play with: one click and it sits down across from you, ready to sign.

The cheering squad

Pets that don't make it to the table (or got benched for another) stand behind you and watch the game. Nobody's left out entirely.

/truco pets

Opens the "Your capybaras" panel with each one's location and mode. come whistles them over from wherever they are; forget lets go of the ones lost for good.

✦ NO NEED TO SIT DOWN ON PURPOSE

Truco out in the world

You don't have to craft a thing to stumble into a game: the world spawns its own rivals.

Wild shacks

A shed with a table and a wild Capybara whose level is fixed for life. Take a seat and the game starts on its own.

2v2 shack

The same shed but with the Big Table and three wild Capybaras waiting: sit on the free side and the 2v2 kicks off right away, with one of them as your partner.

Wandering Capybaras

They roam around looking for somewhere to play. Plant a table nearby and they adopt it: they walk over and wait for a rival. Lose their table, and they wander again.

The Pulpería

Truco's countryside saloon, extremely rare to find: two small tables and a Big Table, with 5 Capybaras of varying difficulty waiting their turn.

✦ MATE ROUND

The kettle and the mate

Craft the kettle from iron and drop it on the campfire, just like food: once it boils, you brew the mate.

A brewed mate holds 2 sips, each giving you Regeneration and Speed — the classic pick-me-up of a good round.

As a bonus: taming with a brewed mate doesn't consume the mate (you still need the prior win, that's non-negotiable), and offering one to a wild Capybara without taming it lowers its distrust for the next game.

✦ IN FOUR STEPS

How to play

Get the table

Craft it from green wool and planks (it's in creative too). For 2v2, build the 2×2 Big Table.

Take a seat

Place it and right-click: you're seated with the sign "X wants to play Truco" waiting for a rival. At the Big Table, the side you pick is your team. Sneak-click an empty table and you play the Capybara directly.

Let a rival sit down

They right-click the same table, accept the invite, and the GUI opens: click a card to play it, buttons for the calls, points declared out loud when the envido is accepted, and hand signs to your partner in 2v2.

Closed it with ESC?

Relax: right-click the table and you're back in the game like nothing happened.

Two things to keep in mind: you declare your envido points out loud, so lying is a choice, not a mistake; and in 2v2 your partner gets (and can have caught) hand signs, not a chat message.

✦ REFERENCE

Commands

Playing does NOT require any command: everything starts by sitting at the table. These are only for checking data or, if you're a server operator, for configuring and debugging.

/truco statsYour record against the Capybara (overall and per level) and your bluffs slipped through, caught, and showdowns won.
/truco last-match [page]The full play-by-play of your last game, paginated 12 lines at a time.
/truco pets [come|forget]With no argument it opens your pets panel. come whistles them over; forget lets go of the ones lost for good.
/truco settings [points|flor|lie|rematch-seconds|pets-limit]Anyone can view the current values. Changing them requires operator OP: target score, with/without flor, point bluffing, rematch seconds and pet limit.
/truco debug [challenge|2v2|accept|decline|reopen|surrender|rematch|bot]Operators OP only, for testing without relying on the world: challenge, set up 2v2, accept/decline, reopen the table, surrender, ask for a rematch or summon the Capybara (bot facil|normal|picante|messi).

Recipes: the table from green wool + planks, the Big Table by joining two tables, and the kettle from iron.

✦ LET'S PLAY

Installation

MINECRAFT 26.2 OR 1.21.1FABRIC LOADERFABRIC APICLIENT + SERVER
  1. Download the jar for your version (there's a build for 26.2 and one for 1.21.1, a separate jar for each) and Fabric API if you don't have it.
  2. Copy both jars into the mods/ folder of your instance and your server.
  3. Craft a Truco Table and challenge someone (or sneak-click to play against the Capybara).

For now distribution is manual, one jar per version — it's not on Modrinth yet.
